Benchmark Methodology & Measurement Protocol

Scale Sizing: Sized across standard scales $N \in [100, 500, 1000, 5000, 10000, 20000]$ to empirically expose asymptotic divergences ($O(N^2)$ quadratic divergence vs $O(N \log N)$ linearithmic scalability vs $O(\log N)$ logarithmic flatness).
